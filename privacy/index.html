Data & control

Local files. Clear boundaries.

Local-first describes where your knowledge lives. The model and services you choose determine where selected context may go.

01 / On your computer

MindOS stores your knowledge in a local directory. You can inspect and edit the files using MindOS or your usual editor. Keep backups before importing or allowing substantial agent edits.

02 / When an agent works

A connected client can use available MCP tools and Skills to access relevant knowledge. Access and actions depend on configuration and the client’s behavior. Review important changes and instructions.

03 / When a cloud model is involved

A cloud model may receive the messages, file excerpts, and tool results needed for the task. Its provider’s policies apply to that data. Local file storage is not a promise that all AI processing stays on your device.

04 / When you enable other services

Configured synchronization and third-party services can send data outside the local directory. Check the settings and destination before enabling them.

05 / Your next decision

Choose a small starting directory, review the agent connection, and use non-sensitive example notes to check the complete workflow before adding important material.
